Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2023

Sudoku-Pi ("we", "our") does not collect or store any personal data. As a Pi Network application, we use the Pi SDK for authentication. We do not have access to your Pi Wallet private keys or personal identity information beyond your Pi username provided by the SDK for gameplay purposes.

We do not share any information with third parties. Your game progress is stored locally on your device or linked to your Pi UID.
